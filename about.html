01 / Who we are

Practical service.
Professional standards.

Obsidian Cleaning is an independent cleaning and hygiene service provider focused on practical service delivery and professional standards.

The offering combines contract cleaning, washroom hygiene management, consumables supply and specialist cleaning. A client can engage Obsidian for an individual service or a coordinated programme shaped around the needs of the property.

Obsidian’s service focus is Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.

06 / The Obsidian standard

Professional service is in the details.

Quality is supported by agreed scopes of work, routine checks, clear communication and periodic service reviews.

Quality & service control

  • Agreed scopes and site-specific schedules.
  • Routine site inspections and documented checklists.
  • Service requests, feedback and corrective-action channels.
  • Consumable monitoring and hygiene servicing records.
  • Periodic client service reviews.

Health, safety & responsible practice

  • Task-appropriate PPE and staff induction.
  • Safe chemical storage, handling and use in line with product instructions.
  • Site risk assessments and appropriate safe-work procedures.
  • Responsible product selection and waste handling.
