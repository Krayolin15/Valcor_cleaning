02 / Scope of service

Industrial cleaning must start with assessment

Services for industrial environments are subject to a site-specific assessment and agreed scope. Do not assume every industrial task or facility is suitable.

01

Warehouses

Discuss cleaning requirements for agreed warehouse areas and staff facilities.

02

Workshops

Site review is important to understand activities, access and cleaning considerations.

03

Selected industrial sites

Suitability is considered on a site-by-site basis.

04

Safety and responsible practice

Appropriate PPE, chemical handling and risk-aware work procedures should be established for the agreed tasks.

03 / Planning & confirmation

Understand the site before the work.

Service frequency, staffing, products, equipment, access arrangements and any site-specific requirements should be confirmed as part of the quotation and service agreement.

  • Assess the property and service requirements.
  • Agree the scope, schedule and relevant resources.
  • Confirm the service arrangement before mobilisation.
  • Review quality, feedback and any corrective actions.
