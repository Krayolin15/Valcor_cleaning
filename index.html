06 / Health, safety & responsible practice

A responsible approach to every site.

Service planning should take account of the work being performed, the site environment and the products or equipment required.

  • Appropriate PPE for assigned tasks and staff induction.
  • Safe storage, handling and use of cleaning chemicals in line with product instructions and applicable requirements.
  • Site risk assessments and safe work procedures appropriate to the service.
  • Responsible product selection and waste handling practices.
