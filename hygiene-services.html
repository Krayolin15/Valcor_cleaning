01 / A managed programme

02 / Hygiene categories

A complete washroom view

Washroom requirements can be organised into four practical categories.

01

Hand Hygiene

  • Soap dispensers and refills.
  • Hand sanitiser dispensers.
  • Paper-towel dispensers and products.
02

Toilet Hygiene

  • Toilet-roll dispensers and tissue.
  • Toilet-seat sanitising systems where suitable.
  • Urinal mats and washroom products.
03

Feminine Hygiene

  • Sanitary bins.
  • Scheduled sanitary waste collection through approved service arrangements.
  • Washroom hygiene checks.
04

Air Care

  • Air-freshener dispensers.
  • Scheduled fragrance refills.
  • Air-care product supply.

03 / Equipment & supply options

Choose the arrangement that fits

Equipment ownership, servicing and consumables can be structured in different ways, subject to the written agreement.

Equipment rental

Obsidian supplies and installs agreed equipment, retains ownership under the rental agreement and charges a recurring fee. Servicing and consumables can be included or itemised separately.

Outright purchase

The client purchases the equipment, with optional installation, maintenance and consumables supply.

Consumables agreement

Products are replenished on an agreed schedule and quantity basis.

Sanitary waste collection is described in the profile as being arranged through approved service arrangements. Confirm the provider, scope and applicable requirements before contracting.
